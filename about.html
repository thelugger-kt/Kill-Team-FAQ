This site is a community-maintained collection of rulings, clarifications, and answers to questions that come up when playing Kill Team but aren't explicitly spelled out in the core rules, Killzone rules, or Faction/Season rules. It's meant to be a quick, mobile-friendly reference you can pull up at the table when a tricky situation comes up during a game.

These rulings are not official Games Workshop errata — they represent the best interpretation of the written rules and the designers' stated intent, arrived at through discussion and testing by the people credited on the Credits page.

Rules Research & Sources

When a ruling is made, we consult the official rules, FAQs, designer commentary, and community discussions to ensure accuracy and fairness. The way this is done is to look for exact verbiage, quotes, and references that support the interpretation being considered. We first will always rely on the text as it is written without making assumptions on why it was written the way it was, this is called Rules as Written (RAW).

Then if a ruling cannot be determined solely from the text, we consider the intent and context provided by the designer commentary or similar rules, this is called Rules as Intended (RAI).

If after that we cannot determine how a rule is meant to function, we will consult the members of the Rulings Panel and others and reach a consensus based on what the most obvious answer appears to be without introducing personal bias or speculation, creating the healthiest outcome for the rule in question and the game itself. This has been coined by Squad Games as Rules as Reasonable (RAR).
